# delegation_ai

## The loop
### Stage 1 — Interview
#### files/01-interview-prompt.md
#### minimum 8 questions, one at a time
#### output: [job-name]/SKILL.md
### Stage 2 — Toolbox
#### files/02-toolbox-prompt.md
#### reusable templates, placeholders, INDEX.md
#### output: [job-name]/files/
### Stage 3 — Proof
#### files/03-proof-prompt.md
#### 5-10 evidence-backed checks before delivery
#### output: Definition of done in SKILL.md

### Never save passwords, unapproved drafts, personal data
### Fix the playbook, not the output
